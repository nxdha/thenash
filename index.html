022024 to 2025Final-year project

DaVinci Minds Learning for ADHD minds

An AI-powered learning platform for students with ADHD. It transcribes YouTube lectures, uses Gemini AI to generate mind maps, notes and MCQs, and tracks attention in real time with eye-tracking and computer vision. When focus drops, it brings students back with games and soft audio cues, and a parent portal shows progress and focus trends.

  • Computer vision
